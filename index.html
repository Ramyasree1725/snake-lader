How to Play & Support

📖 Game Rules & Board Map

  • All players start at position 0 (off-board) and race to 100.
  • Click Roll Dice (or press Space) to roll 1–6.
  • Land on a Ladder base to climb straight up to the top cell!
  • Land on a Snake head and slide down to its tail.
  • Exact 100 Rule: If your roll goes past 100, you stay on your current tile.
  • Bonus 6: When enabled in Settings, rolling a 6 grants an extra turn (3 consecutive 6s pass the turn).
